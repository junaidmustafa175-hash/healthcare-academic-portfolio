# Teaching Philosophy

My approach centres on applied, student-centred learning that bridges theory and practice in healthcare management and public health. I design curricula that prioritise critical thinking, evidence-based decision-making, and real-world operational challenges.

Key elements:
- Module leadership with clear learning outcomes aligned to professional standards
- Inclusive assessment strategies (formative and summative)
- Effective use of virtual learning environments (Canvas, Moodle)
- Mentorship and pastoral support to drive student success and progression
- Alignment with Advance HE / HEA framework for teaching excellence

I am committed to continuous improvement of teaching practice and to preparing graduates for leadership roles in healthcare systems.
